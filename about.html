about us

interior points nepal brief service intro

Located in Baneshwor, Sankhamul-Marg, Kathmandu Nepal. Interior Points is a full-service architectural design firm that takes an eco approach to its invents array of projects, with a desire to create exceptionally LUXE environments. Its innovative architecture, interior design and products firm specializing in high-end residential, hospitality and home product design.

We offer an interior design service that includes all furniture and fixtures, soft furnishing, curtains, cushions and accessories. we can help with the overall design scheme including rooms layout, maximizing room functions, color schemes, mood boards and fabric selection. Our team undertake projects of all sizes and budgets, from a full interior to a single room. We respect the entire client’s requirement and commit to an ultimate satisfaction. we aim to create an excellent design and provide excellent service to meet our clients time frame and budget.

Our flexible and manageable team can help and provide suitable solutions to meet with each clients niche. The firm is practicing Interior Design and Construction with the experienced manpower and modern draughting and computer equipment using AutoCAD Ver 2010, Revit Architecture and other related software. The firm has its own office space centrally located at Baneshwor, Sankhamul-Marg, Kathmandu, Nepal.

our vision

To establish Interior Points Nepal as the premier interior design firm in Nepal, renowned for transforming spaces into inspring, functional, and sustainable environments that seamlessly blend the rich cultural heritage of Nepal with contemporary design innovation. We aspire to set the standard for excellence in the industry, creating timeless spaces that elevate lifestyle, foster creativity, and promote harmony between tradition and modernity.

our mission

To deliver innovative, high-quality interior design solutions that blend creativity, functionality, and sustainable, while prioritizing client satisfaction, cultural sensitivity, and environmental responsibility. We aim to enhance tha lives of our clients by crafting spaces that are both aesthetically pleading and practical, fostering a sense of pride and comfort in every project.

how we work

initial consultantion

We begin with a personalized meeting to discuss your style, preferences, and specific needs for the space.

design planing

This phase includes choosing color schemes, textures, decor elements, and creating detailed mood boards and 3D visualizations.

project execution

With the design finalized, we oversee the procurement, installation, and coordination of all decorative elements and furnishings.

final review

Upon completion, we conduct a comprehensive review with you to ensure the space meets your vision and expectations.

FAQs

What is Interior Points Nepal

Interior Points Nepal, officially "INTERIOR POINTS Pvt. Ltd.," is a premier interior design and construction firm established in 2006. With over 16 years of experience, we specialize in creating innovative and functional designs for offices, banks, residential, and corporate buildings across Nepal.

What makes Interior Points Nepal different from other interior design firms?

Our 16 years of experience, combined with a team of skilled architects, interior designers, and technical staff, sets us apart. We use modern equipment and the latest technological know-how to deliver high-quality, site-specific designs with a focus on quality control and client satisfaction.

Who are the professionals behind Interior Points Nepal's project?

Our team consists of experienced architects, interior designers, overseers, draughtsmen, and supporting staff. Each member is highly qualified and dedicated to delivering innovative and practical design solutions tailored to your needs.

How does Interior Points Nepal ensure quality in its projects?

We prioritize quality control by using advanced equipment, adhering to technical specifications, and leveraging our team’s expertise. Every project is customized to the site’s conditions and client requirements, ensuring durability and aesthetic excellence.
